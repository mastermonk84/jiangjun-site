# Privacy Policy — Jiangjun 将军

_Last updated: August 13, 2026_

Jiangjun 将军 ("the app") is a single-player game. It is designed to work
entirely on your device.

## Data collection

The app collects **no data**. It has no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, no tracking, and it makes no network requests. Nothing you
do in the app leaves your device.

## Local storage

Your campaign progress, settings, and the Hall of Fallen Heroes are saved
only in the app's local storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes
this data. It is never transmitted anywhere.

## Third parties

The app uses no third-party services, SDKs, or data processors.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above, this policy
will be updated and the change noted in the version's release notes.
